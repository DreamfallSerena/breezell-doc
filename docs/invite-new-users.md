# How to Invite New Users

Entanglement and Teams are included in every paid plan. On the Free plan you can unlock them **permanently**: five Breezell users assist you through your personal invite link. This page walks you through the whole process as a series of short tutorials, from signing in to using the modes.

::: tip About the screens on this page
The screens are rebuilt in HTML from the source code of Breezell and breezell.com, and some of their buttons can be clicked. breezell.com is only available in English, so its screens are in English in every language of this guide, while the Breezell screens follow the language of this page. The bar at the top of each website screen shows the address you are on. Names, email addresses, links and codes in the screens are examples.
:::

## How unlocking works {#rules}

- **Five assists** unlock Entanglement and Teams. The unlock is permanent: it stays with your account whatever plan you have later.
- **A paid plan** includes both modes while it is active, with no assists needed. This covers any plan other than Free, the pay-once Custom API licence and a seat on an active team. If the plan ends before you have five assists, the modes lock again.
- **Each Breezell account can assist only one person.** Assisting twice for the same person does not count twice.
- **Each device can be used for one assist only**, whichever account is signed in on it.
- **You cannot assist yourself.**
- **An invite link is valid for 30 days.** Generating a new link makes the previous one stop working; the assists you already have are kept.
- **The unlock belongs to your Breezell account.** Use the same account on breezell.com and in the editor.

The unlock card lives in your account settings on breezell.com.

1. Open [breezell.com](https://breezell.com) and sign in with the **same account** as in the editor. Click your avatar at the top right, then **Profile**.

2. In your account, click **Settings** in the left sidebar. You can also open the menu with your name at the bottom of the sidebar and click **Account settings**:

<BzWangzhan ye="yonghucaidan" gaoliang="zhanghushezhi" biaoji="2" />

3. Settings shows your account details, your team, the unlock card **Unlock Entanglement and Teams Modes** and the sign-out option. The card shows how many assists you have:

<BzWangzhan ye="shezhi" :jindu="0" gaoliang="ka" biaoji="3" />

## Tutorial 3: Generate and copy your invite link {#generate}

1. On the unlock card, click **Generate invite link**.

<BzWangzhan ye="shezhi" :jindu="0" gaoliang="shengcheng" biaoji="1" />

2. Your link appears below the buttons. The first button changes to **Regenerate**, and **Copy link** appears next to it. Click **Copy link**; the button shows **Copied** for a moment. Try it here:

<BzWangzhan ye="shezhi" :jindu="0" lianjie gaoliang="fuzhi" biaoji="2" />

3. The clipboard now holds a short message and your link:

```text
"Can you help me unlock this? I'm waiting in the Breezell editor."
https://breezell.com/mode-invite/entanglement?token=YOUR_INVITE_TOKEN
```

The token above is a placeholder: send the text your own account generates.

::: warning Generate once, then keep the text
The card shows your link only until you leave the page. When you come back, it offers **Generate invite link** again, and generating creates a **new** link: the link you already sent stops working. Keep the copied text, and send the same link to all five friends. Generate a new one only if your link has expired after 30 days or reached people it should not have.
:::

## Tutorial 4: Share the link {#share}

- Paste the copied text into any chat app or email and send it to five people.
- Each friend needs their own Breezell account. Registering is free and can be done straight from the link.
- Each friend should use their own computer or phone: a device that has already been used for an assist cannot assist again.
- The invite page shows your name shortened to its first and last letter, for example **A\*\*\*x** for Alex. Tell your friends the link is from you, so they recognize it.
- Anyone who has the link can assist you, so send it to people you know.
- The invite page is in English. You can send your friends the link to [Tutorial 5](#assist) so they can follow along in their language.

## Tutorial 5: Assist as a friend {#assist}

This tutorial is for the friends you invite: it shows what they see after opening your link.

### Step 1: Open the link {#assist-open}

The invite page names the person who invited you. If you are not signed in to breezell.com, it asks you to sign in or register first:

<BzWangzhan ye="yaoqing" zhuangtai="weidenglu" gaoliang="zhuce" biaoji="1" />

- **Register** if you do not have a Breezell account yet, and continue with step 2.
- **Sign in** if you do. Sign in with your email and password or with GitHub, and continue with step 3.
- If you are already signed in, the page shows the **Assist** button directly. Continue with step 3.

### Step 2: Create an account {#assist-register}

**Register** opens **Create Account**. Registration takes three steps, shown by the numbers at the top. Click **Next** and **Previous** to go through them here:

<BzWangzhan ye="denglu" zhuangtai="zhuce" laiyuan="yaoqing" gaoliang="xiayibu" biaoji="2" />

1. **Username**: choose a username, then click **Next**.
2. **Email** and **Verification Code**: enter your email and click **Get Code**. Enter the code from the email you receive, then click **Next**.
3. **Password** and **Confirm Password**: the password needs at least 8 characters, an uppercase letter, a number and a special character. Click **Create Account**.

You can also skip the form with **Continue with GitHub**. After signing in or registering, the page shows **Success!** and takes you back to the invite page about a second later.

### Step 3: Click Assist {#assist-click}

Back on the invite page, click **Assist**:

<BzWangzhan ye="yaoqing" gaoliang="zhuli" biaoji="3" />

### Step 4: Done {#assist-done}

The page confirms the assist and shows the new progress of the person who invited you, here 3 of 5:

<BzWangzhan ye="yaoqing" zhuangtai="chenggong" :jindu="3" />

**View my account** opens your own account page. If you open the link again later, the page says **You already completed this assist**.

The unlock card updates itself every 15 seconds and whenever you come back to the browser tab. To check right away, click the refresh button at the top right of the card.

Open the mode menu under the chat input and hover **Entanglement** or **Teams**. Next to the menu you see <BzWenben jian="chatMode.entanglementUnlockTitle" /> and your progress, here <BzWenben jian="chatMode.entanglementUnlockProgress" canshu="3|5" />.

<BzLiaotian tanchu="moshi" moshi="agent" suoding :jiesuojindu="3" />

The card shows a green check and **Entanglement and Teams modes are unlocked**. The progress bar and the invite buttons disappear.

## Tutorial 7: Use Entanglement and Teams {#use}

1. In Breezell, open the mode menu under the chat input. Opening it checks your unlock status, and the locks disappear.
2. Choose **Entanglement** or **Teams**.

<BzLiaotian tanchu="moshi" moshi="entanglement" />

To learn how the modes work, see [Entanglement Mode](/entanglement-mode-overview) and [Teams](/mode-selection#teams). If the modes are still locked, check the account card at the bottom of the Box sidebar: it must show the account you unlocked them with (see [Tutorial 1](#sign-in-editor)). Then close the mode menu and open it again.

## Messages and fixes {#messages}

### On the invite page {#messages-invite}

| Message | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **This invite link is invalid or expired** | The link is incomplete, older than 30 days, or was replaced by a newer link. | Ask for the current link. |
| **Each account can assist only one unlock** | This account has already assisted someone else. | Another friend has to assist. |
| **You already completed this assist** | This account has already assisted this person. The assist counts. | Nothing. |
| **You cannot assist yourself** | The link was opened with the account it belongs to. | Send the link to friends. |
| **This user already completed the unlock** | The person who invited you has already unlocked both modes. | Nothing. |
| **This device has already completed an unlock assist** | An assist has already been made on this device, possibly with another account. | Assist from a different device. |
| **Unable to verify this device. Refresh the page and try again.** | The page could not check the device. | Refresh the page and try again, or try another browser. |

## Why invitations {#why}

Breezell needs ongoing funding for its infrastructure, maintenance and development. Inviting friends is a way to unlock the advanced modes without a plan while helping Breezell grow. Thank you for your support. For what changed in version 1.2.8, see [Version 1.2.8 Access Update](/version-1-2-8-access-update).
