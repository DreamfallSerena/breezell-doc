# How to Invite New Users

Breezell provides a personal invitation link that you can share with new users. When eligible invited users complete registration and accept your invitation, their progress can count toward unlocking Entanglement and Teams.

## Before you begin

Make sure you have a Breezell account and are signed in on the official website.

## Open the website and profile menu

1. Open the official Breezell website: [https://breezell.com](https://breezell.com).
2. Sign in to your Breezell account if needed.
3. Click your profile avatar in the top-right corner.

![Breezell website with the profile avatar highlighted](/invitation-guide/open-profile-menu.png)

## Open your Profile page

1. In the account menu, find **Profile**.
2. Click **Profile** to open your profile page.

![Account menu with Profile highlighted](/invitation-guide/select-profile.png)

## Generate an invitation link

1. On the Profile page, select **Account** in the left sidebar.
2. Find the **Unlock Entanglement and Teams Modes** section.
3. Click **Generate invite link**.

![Account page with the Generate invite link button](/invitation-guide/generate-invite-link.png)

## Send the invitation to a friend

After the link is generated, Breezell provides a message similar to this:

```text
Can you help me unlock this? I'm waiting in the Breezell editor.
https://breezell.com/mode-invite/entanglement?token=YOUR_INVITE_TOKEN
```

The token above is only a placeholder. Copy the complete message and link generated by your own account, then send them to your friend.

Your friend can open the link, register or sign in, and accept the invitation. Eligible new users count toward your unlock progress after completing the required registration flow.
