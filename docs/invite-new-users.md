# How to Invite New Users

Entanglement and Teams are included in every paid plan. On the Free plan you can unlock them **permanently** by getting five Breezell users to assist you through your personal invite link.

::: tip About the screens on this page
The screens below are rebuilt in HTML from the source code of breezell.com and of Breezell. The website is available in Chinese and English, so its screens show Chinese on the Chinese pages of this guide and English everywhere else, just like the real site.
:::

## Before you begin {#before}

- You need a Breezell account. Sign in on [breezell.com](https://breezell.com) with the same account you use in the editor.
- The friends who assist you need their own Breezell account. They can register while accepting your invitation.

## 1. Open your account settings {#open}

Sign in on [breezell.com](https://breezell.com) and click your avatar at the top right, then **Profile**.

<BzWangzhan ye="caidan" gaoliang="profile" biaoji="1" />

In your account, click **Settings** in the left sidebar. You can also go there directly from the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar (**Account settings**).

## 2. Generate your invite link {#generate}

Settings shows the unlock card with your progress. Click the button to generate your link:

<BzWangzhan ye="shezhi" :jindu="2" gaoliang="shengcheng" biaoji="2" />

Once the link exists, the card shows it, and the buttons change to regenerate the link and to copy it. Try it here:

<BzWangzhan ye="shezhi" :jindu="2" lianjie gaoliang="fuzhi" biaoji="3" />

Copying puts a short message and your link on the clipboard:

```text
"Can you help me unlock this? I'm waiting in the Breezell editor."
https://breezell.com/mode-invite/entanglement?token=YOUR_INVITE_TOKEN
```

The token above is a placeholder: send the text your own account generates.

## 3. Your friend assists you {#assist}

Your friend opens the link. If they are not signed in, they sign in or register first:

<BzWangzhan ye="yaoqing" zhuangtai="weidenglu" />

Then they confirm with one click:

<BzWangzhan ye="yaoqing" gaoliang="zhuli" biaoji="1" />

The page confirms the assist and shows your new progress, for example 3/5.

<BzWangzhan ye="yaoqing" zhuangtai="chenggong" />

### Rules for assists {#rules}

- Five assists unlock Entanglement and Teams permanently for your account.
- Each account can assist only one unlock. A friend who already helped someone else sees this message:

<BzWangzhan ye="yaoqing" zhuangtai="yizhuli" />

- Each device can be used for one assist only.
- You cannot assist yourself.
- Any paid plan includes both modes, with no assists needed.

## 4. Check your progress {#progress}

Your progress is shown on the unlock card in Settings, and in the editor: open the mode menu under the chat input and hover Entanglement or Teams.

<BzLiaotian tanchu="moshi" moshi="agent" suoding />

When the fifth assist arrives, the lock disappears and both modes can be chosen. If the website cannot read your unlock status, the card says so and offers a retry button; signing out and in again also helps.

## Why invitations {#why}

Breezell needs ongoing funding for its infrastructure, maintenance and development. Inviting friends is a way to unlock the advanced modes without a plan while helping Breezell grow. Thank you for your support. For what changed in version 1.2.8, see [Version 1.2.8 Access Update](/version-1-2-8-access-update).
